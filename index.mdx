Focaldata AI is an end-to-end research platform that takes teams from question to decision-ready insight fast, without needing an in-house research department. It conducts AI-led qualitative interviews with real participants, then synthesises the results into evidence-linked reporting you can trace back to what people actually said.

It combines qualitative methodology with strict quality controls and automation, so you get reliable data from real audiences in 24–72 hours, with reporting built in.

## What it is not

Focaldata AI is not a general-purpose chatbot, and it is not a substitute for representative measurement. It is optimised for rapid, structured qualitative work where traceability from claim to evidence matters. It is not designed to replace representative measurement or statistical inference, pricing optimisation or demand forecasting, or clinical, legal, and compliance decisions that require licensed professional standards.

<Note>
  AI accelerates research production and improves consistency, but it does not remove professional responsibility. You remain accountable for objectives, interpretation, and any client-facing conclusions.
</Note>

## About Focaldata

Focaldata is a research technology company founded in 2017, built to help organisations run high-quality social and market research at scale. Our platforms support research in 130 markets, and in 2025 we conducted research with approximately seven million respondents worldwide. The platform is in active use with major organisations and leading creative and strategic agencies, and is developed by a cross-functional researcher–engineer team with research leadership spanning public opinion, survey methodology, and qualitative practice.
