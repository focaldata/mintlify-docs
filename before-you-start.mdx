Use Arlo when you need fast, decision-oriented qualitative insight. The platform is strongest when the objective is clear, the audience is straightforwardly recruitable, and the output you need is a set of decision-ready recommendations grounded in verbatim evidence.

## Quick scoping checklist

Use this to scope a study in under 10 minutes. It captures the minimum needed to generate a defensible brief, confirm recruitability, and align the design to the decision it supports.

- **Decision** — can you state the decision this research will support in one sentence?
- **Audience** — is the target audience recruitable with realistic criteria and incidence?
- **Method fit** — is the question best answered qualitatively (logic, language, drivers) rather than by representative measurement?
- **Stimulus** — do you have the stimulus ready, and is it appropriate for the chosen method?
- **Timeline** — is the turnaround consistent with recruitment realities and review checkpoints?

## May need additional consideration

Some work is still appropriate but often benefits from specialist input or a managed approach:

- Hard-to-reach or highly specialised audiences (e.g. niche B2B roles in specific markets).
- Multi-market programmes requiring strict cross-market comparability.
- Highly sensitive topics (e.g. mental health, trauma, minors) where additional governance and review may be necessary.
- Studies with complex stimulus or advanced experimental design requirements.
- Client contexts with heightened scrutiny (e.g. regulated sectors) requiring expanded method documentation.

<Tip>
  In these cases, involve a research specialist or your Focaldata account team early.
</Tip>

## Not designed for

Arlo is not designed to replace:

- Representative measurement, forecasting, or statistical inference without an explicitly designed quant approach.
- Pricing optimisation, elasticity modelling, or demand forecasting.
- Clinical, diagnostic, or medical decision-making requiring licensed professional standards.
- Legal advice or compliance determinations.
- Unrestricted freeform interviewing without an agreed guide and scope.

<Note>
  Where client questions imply these needs, Arlo can still contribute as an input — but should not be the primary method.
</Note>
