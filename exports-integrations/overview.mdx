Once a study is complete, you can take its outputs out of the platform for client delivery and downstream use.

## Downloading project documents

Every project document can be downloaded from the platform. Select the download button on a document and choose a format:

- **Word document (.docx)**: a formatted, editable file, ready to share with stakeholders or mark up.
- **Plain text**: the document content without formatting.

This covers the brief, audience definition, questionnaire or discussion guide, and Cost & Timeline.

<Note>
  Other export formats and integration options are being documented. In the meantime, your Focaldata account team can walk you through the routes available for your workspace.
</Note>

## Sharing outputs responsibly

When moving data out of the platform, apply the same data hygiene that governs the project itself:

- Prefer curated outputs — memos, decks, quote packs — over raw transcripts or full exports.
- Respect privacy, PII rules, and NDAs in anything you share externally.
- Be deliberate about where outputs are copied or pasted.
